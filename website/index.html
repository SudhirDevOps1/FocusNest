20 • NO MARKETING HYPE

Honest Engineering Disclosures & Platform Limits

Local DNS vs Private DNS / DoH

FocusNestVpnService intercepts plain UDP/53 queries locally. When Android Private DNS (DNS-over-TLS) or browser DNS-over-HTTPS is enabled, those encrypted lookups bypass port 53. Android also permits only one active VPN app at a time.

Reels/Shorts, Grayscale & OEM Savers

Short-form view-ID interception is optional and fails safely without breaking the host app if third-party layouts change. System grayscale cannot be forced without privileged WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS, so FocusNest links directly to Android Display & Bedtime settings.
