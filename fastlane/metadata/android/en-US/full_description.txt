FocusNest is a 100% free, privacy-first, open-source Android digital wellbeing companion.

- Real screen-time analytics via Android UsageStatsManager
- Pomodoro, Countdown & Stopwatch focus sessions with reboot persistence
- Configurable app limits, shared app groups, and priority-ordered profiles
- 11 unlock challenges including offline QR/Barcode scanning, NFC tags, Adaptive Math, and Intention Entry
- Optional on-device VpnService DNS website filter and configurable Reels/Shorts pause prompts
- Encrypted AES-256-GCM local backups and CSV/JSON/Printable reports

No ads, no trackers, no accounts, and no internet permission.
