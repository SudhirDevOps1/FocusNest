# FocusNest Features (`v1.2.0`)

- **Home Dashboard (`AdaptiveTwoPane`)**: Time-of-day localized greeting, yesterday-vs-today screen time comparison delta, daily screen time vs goal ring, remaining allowance, top apps with icons & daily cap shares, quick focus, quick shield, Quick Habits action, active profiles, blocked attempts, weekly comparison chart, and daily intention.
- **Focus Sanctuary & Offline Ambient Soundscapes**: Pomodoro, Countdown, and Stopwatch modes, 6 session types, duration presets, `+5m` Extend, Restart, Break Skip, custom breaks/cycles, streaks, monotonic elapsed-realtime clock guard, reboot persistence, and procedural zero-network `AudioTrack` soundscapes (`Brown Noise`, `Soft Rain`, `Warm Drone`, `Study Wash`).
- **Mindful App-Launch Pause Curtain**: Arm individual apps with an optional `5s–15s` guided breathing pause before opening (`LaunchPauseCoordinator` + 5-minute proceed cooldown), plus an instant preview button in the Blocker tab.
- **App, Category & Profile Blocker**: Per-app daily/weekly limits with configurable grace periods, category daily caps, shared app groups, 80%/90%/100% warning notifications, `Study`/`College`/`Work`/`Sleep`/`Reading`/`Weekend`/`Custom` profiles, recurring schedules, emergency whitelist, and deterministic priority resolution.
- **12 Unlock Challenges**: Breathing Challenge (4-2-4 orb), Strict Mode, Fixed Cooldown, Temporary Unlock, Offline QR Scan (one-time & timed replay-safe tokens), Offline Barcode Scan, NFC Tag Tap, Adaptive Math (with division), Sentence Typing, Personal Intention (with quick chips), Daily Unlock Limit, and Reflection Pause.
- **Insights, Weekly PDF Report Card & Exports**: Real `UsageStatsManager` metrics across Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Custom date ranges, category breakdowns, Longest Session & Best Day comparisons, styled Printable/PDF Wellbeing Report Card generator, plus CSV and JSON exports.
- **Daily Habits & Evening Wind-Down**: Offline Room-backed Daily & Weekly Habit Tracker with streaks and 7-day charts, paired with a daily auto-resetting Bedtime Wind-Down Checklist, Scheduled Do Not Disturb, Break reminders, and one-tap shortcuts to Android Display Brightness and Night Light settings.
- **Website & Reels Filtering**: Local-only `VpnService` DNS sinkhole with per-app network scoping and optional Reels/Shorts pause reduction.
- **Invincible Mode & Hardware Integrations**: Delayed rule changes, BiometricPrompt gate, owner recovery, 3 Home Screen widgets, Android Quick Settings Tile (`Focus Shield`), NFC & offline QR/Barcode vault, 6 accent palettes, Light/Dark/System themes, Dynamic Color, English & Hindi (`hi`) runtime switching, and zero-internet browser handoff to GitHub Releases.
